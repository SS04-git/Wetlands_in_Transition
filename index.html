    Manual and automated tracing

    Can a model trace what was traced by hand?

    A classifier learnt the traced classes from the satellite images and the hand-traced polygons. Each date shown here was classified by a model that never saw it: it learnt only from the other sixteen dates.

    Random forest (scikit-learn) on the colour and texture of every 0.5 m cell. Below, the tracing; above, the model's version of the same image.

    Model · Held-out date

    Shown first: the date with the median accuracy of the seventeen, neither the best nor the worst.

    Model · Where they differ

    The edge between land and water is not a line.

    It moves with the tide and the seasons. Planning and mapping need to acknowledge the transitional nature of wetlands, rather than impose permanent boundaries on a landscape that is inherently fluid.
