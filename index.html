Wetlands in Transition

Mapping change and development in the Panchagangavali estuary, Kundapur, Karnataka.

Scroll to descend

13.639° N, 74.692° E

Where the Panchagangavali meets the Arabian Sea.

The estuary is a working landscape for the Kharvi community, who fish, collect clams and sort shellfish along its edges.

No usable image was found for the monsoon.

Rain and cloud hide the estuary from June to September. Every observation in this study comes from the drier months.
